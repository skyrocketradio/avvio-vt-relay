    Mic not detected?
    • Open the site in Safari or Chrome directly — not an embedded/in-app browser.
    • macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone — enable your browser, then re-check.
    • When the browser asks to use the mic, choose Allow. If blocked, click the site-info icon in the address bar and re-allow.
    • Pick your RODE above — the browser may default to the built-in mic.

    How the editor works

    • ← Outro — audition the end of the song you're coming out of.
    • ↑ Record — start talking over the outro.
    • → Next — drop the next song in under you.
    • ↓ Stop — end the take.
    • Drag the green VT or the outro to set where you talk; drag the incoming song to set where it starts.
    • The gold POST line is where the next song's vocal begins — talk up to it.
    • Click the timeline to place the playhead. Space plays / stops from there.
    • Enter twice sends the take to the station (first Enter confirms).
    • Scroll to zoom (or −/+ / Fit); drag empty space to pan.
    • Set mic, speaker & music duck level under 🎙 Audio.
